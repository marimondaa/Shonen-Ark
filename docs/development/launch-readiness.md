## Operator runbook to complete (not claimed operational)

1. Designate a privacy lead and monitored public contact. Requests must work without account access. Record receipt, request type, due date and assignee in a restricted log. Verify identity proportionately through existing contact/account context; do not routinely collect ID scans, full birth dates or passwords.
2. For access/correction/portability, locate Auth and all `ark_*` records by verified account ID, including contact data; produce a structured export, exclude other people's private information, deliver securely and record response. Québec access/rectification timing: normally 30 days according to CAI guidance; have counsel validate application and refusal handling.
3. For deletion, check any documented legal hold/necessary retention, explain retained categories/reasons, then use reviewed administrator procedures. Account FK cascades in `mvp.sql` cover these new tables; legacy tables, provider logs and backups require separate handling. Confirm completion, log minimal evidence, prevent restored backups from silently resurrecting deleted live data. No instant-everywhere promise.
4. Approve category-specific retention durations and implement purge jobs only after decisions. Separate incident/rights request records, active service data and provider backups. Backups are not a reason to retain live records indefinitely.
5. Complete an EFVP for this new electronic service, assess any processing outside Québec before transferring data, confirm supplier safeguards/contracts/regions and limit administrator access with MFA. An SQL schema is not an EFVP.
6. Incident response: contain access, preserve minimal evidence, assess sensitivity/consequences/likelihood, involve privacy lead, document all incidents. Notify CAI and affected people where serious-injury risk requires it; incident register entries must be retained at least five years under CAI guidance. Have counsel validate additional foreign notice obligations.
7. Rights/UGC complaints: publish a reachable reporting contact, collect URL and specific allegation with only necessary contact data, record triage and decisions, prevent disclosure of complainant details, give an appropriate response/appeal path. Do not represent Canadian hosting as US DMCA immunity.

## Jurisdictions and official sources (consulted 2026-09-26)

- **Québec private-sector privacy** is the primary review framework given the operator location and planned accounts. CAI explains necessity, consent, governance, technology assessments, cross-border communication and retention: [governance/EFVP](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/responsable-protection-renseignements-personnels-entreprise), [collection](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/collecte-renseignements-personnels_entreprises), [communication/transfers](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/utilisation-communication-renseignements-personnels), [retention](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/conservation-destruction-renseignements-personnels). Law text fetch failed during review; professional review must check current legislation itself.
- **Younger users:** CAI describes parental/tutor consent for under-14s with limited exceptions; a birthday checkbox does not implement this. Options for owner decision: initially a clearly defined older audience with proportionate age assurance, or a designed parental-consent/minor-safety workflow. Avoid collecting full DOB/identity documents by default. [CAI consent](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/consentement-personnes-entreprises).
- **Québec French:** complete French website/services and French-first adhesion-contract handling must be assessed, not replaced by a language-choice checkbox. [OQLF service/site rights](https://www.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/francisation/droits_linguistiques/droits/langue-du-commerce-et-des-affaires.html), [current contract guidance](https://www.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/francisation/entreprises/contrats-adhesion.html).
- **Canada:** PIPEDA can apply to interprovincial/international commercial transactions despite substantially similar Québec law. Actual customer/processing footprint needs confirmation. [OPC jurisdiction guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/r_o_p/02_05_d_26/). CASL classification should consider actual message purpose; necessary auth email does not authorize marketing. [CRTC FAQ](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm).
- **United States:** English/global anime discovery and accessible signup make US audience plausible, not confirmed. Assess COPPA if directed to under-13s or actual knowledge of their data; the FTC identifies a 2025 amended rule, so do not rely on old FAQs alone. State privacy/consumer thresholds need business and audience facts. [FTC guidance and revised-rule link](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions).
- **EEA:** GDPR Article 3 targeting/monitoring tests need actual audience/marketing analysis; mere worldwide accessibility does not establish application. If applicable, assess legal bases, rights, transfers and Article 8 child-consent thresholds (13–16 by member state). [Official GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). UK audience would require separate UK GDPR/PECR and children's-design review; no UK targeting is established yet.
- **Incidents:** [CAI private-sector incident guidance](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/protection-renseignements-personnels/information-entreprises-privees/incidents-confidentialite-mesures-securite-entreprises).
- **Accessibility:** [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), engineering target only, not certification or determination of every jurisdiction's accessibility law.
- **Catalog:** [AniList official terms](https://anilist.gitbook.io/anilist-apiv2-docs/docs/guide/terms-of-use) restrict mass collection and competing list/tracker services; commercial use above the documented revenue threshold requires a license. Resolve exact threshold/currency and whether this product is complementary before monetizing. API access is not blanket artwork clearance.
